## Project Overview

**BolaoAoVivo** is a web-based FIFA World Cup 2026 Simulator ("Simulador da Copa do Mundo 2026") in Portuguese. The app allows users to simulate tournament results across the group stage and knockout bracket.

A UI mockup of the intended design is at `picture/tela_de_captura.png`. It shows:
- **Group stage**: All groups (A–H/L) with team standings, match scores, and result indicators
- **Knockout bracket**: Elimination rounds from Round of 32 down to the Final
- **News section**: Football news feed with article cards and photos
